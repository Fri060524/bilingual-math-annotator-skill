# Academic Translation Guide for Mathematics & STEM Textbooks

This guide specifies the translation philosophy, style rules, and terminological standards for producing bilingual editions of advanced mathematics textbooks.

---

## 1. Core Principles

1. **Mathematical Fidelity Over Literary Flow**:
   - Theorems, hypotheses, bounds, and inferences must be preserved with 100% mathematical precision.
   - Do not embellish or add extraneous logical premises not present in the original text.
2. **Preservation of Proof Architecture**:
   - Maintain the author's logical sequence step by step. Do not condense multi-step deductions into casual summaries.
3. **Natural Chinese Academic Voice**:
   - Use standard Chinese higher-mathematics terminology (complying with China National Committee for Terms in Natural Sciences / 全国科学技术名词审定委员会).
   - Avoid awkward machine-translation phrasing and convoluted Europeanized sentences.

---

## 2. Modality & Logical Connectives (论证语力对照规范)

English mathematical writing relies heavily on specific auxiliary words to convey logical force. These must be translated systematically:

| English Academic Pattern | Preferred Chinese Translation | Prohibited / Discouraged Translation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `clearly`, `obviously`, `evidently` | 显然 | 很明显 / 明白地 |
| `it follows that`, `consequently` | 由此可知 / 从而 | 它接着是 / 结果是 |
| `in other words`, `that is` | 换言之 / 亦即 | 也就是说（过于口语） |
| `more precisely` | 更精确地说 | 更准地 |
| `without loss of generality` | 不失一般性 | 没有失去一般性 |
| `by virtue of`, `in view of` | 鉴于 / 依据 / 由...可知 | 凭借着它的美德 |
| `appeal to` (a lemma/theorem) | 援引 / 借助于 | 呼吁 / 吸引 |
| `pass to the limit` | 取极限 / 转向极限 | 传递到极限 |
| `be bounded above/below by` | 有上界 / 下界为 / 由...界定 | 被...在上被限制 |
| `contrary to our hypothesis` | 与假设矛盾 | 相反于我们的假说 |

## 3. Mathematical Predicates and Concepts (数学概念与谓词严格区分)

Ensure rigorous distinction between mathematical concepts:

- **Quantifiers**:
  - `for all`, `for any`, `arbitrary` $\to$ 任意 / 对任意
  - `there exists`, `for some` $\to$ 存在 / 对某个
- **Logical Conditions**:
  - `necessary and sufficient` $\to$ 充要条件 / 当且仅当
  - `sufficient condition` $\to$ 充分条件
  - `necessary condition` $\to$ 必要条件
- **Measure Theory & Analysis Nuances**:
  - `almost everywhere (a.e.)` $\to$ 几乎处处（绝不能遗漏“处处”）
  - `almost disjoint` $\to$ 几乎不相交（指相交部分测度为零，绝不可直接写成“不相交”）
  - `disjoint` $\to$ 不相交（交集为空）
  - `pointwise convergence` $\to$ 逐点收敛
  - `uniform convergence` $\to$ 一致收敛
  - `weak convergence` $\to$ 弱收敛
- **Enunciation Types**:
  - `Theorem` $\to$ 定理
  - `Lemma` $\to$ 引理
  - `Proposition` $\to$ 命题
  - `Corollary` $\to$ 推论
  - `Definition` $\to$ 定义
  - `Remark` $\to$ 注记
  - `Proof` $\to$ 证明
  - `Observation` $\to$ 观察
  - `Claim` $\to$ 断言

## 4. Academic Margin Annotation Principles (页边注释选词准则)

Academic annotations in the outer margin serve dual purposes:
1. Clarifying complex technical terms.
2. Training readers in authentic English mathematical writing.

### Selection Criteria:
- **Include**:
  - Core domain terminology (e.g., `exterior measure`, `sigma-algebra`, `orthonormal basis`).
  - Formal academic collocations (e.g., `appeal to`, `be uniformly bounded by`, `pass to the limit`).
  - Rhetorical/logical transition markers in proof writing.
- **Strictly Exclude**:
  - Common general English vocabulary (e.g., `important`, `begin`, `result`, `book`, `study`).
- **Density Control**:
  - Maximum 3–5 annotated terms per printed page to prevent visual crowding.
